Project Objectives

Intelligent Vision-Based Crowd Monitoring & Public Safety Risk Detection
01
Real-Time AI Crowd Monitoring
Develop a real-time AI-based crowd monitoring system using video surveillance for effective crowd analysis across live camera feeds, CCTV streams, and uploaded footage.
02
Accurate Person Detection & Counting
Detect and count people accurately in crowded environments using YOLOv8 and OpenCV, providing per-frame tracking IDs and real-time occupancy metrics.
03
Crowd Density Estimation
Estimate crowd density levels — Low Medium High — based on detected crowd size relative to zone capacity, displayed via a 3×4 grid overlay.
04
Abnormal Crowd Behaviour Analysis
Analyze crowd movement patterns and identify abnormal behaviour — including sudden directional shifts, high-velocity dispersion, and clustering collapse — that may indicate an unsafe situation.
05
Stampede Risk Prediction & Real-Time Alerts
Predict potential stampede risks using a multi-modal risk engine combining density, violence, panic, noise, and weapon signals. Generate real-time alerts with evidence snapshots to improve public safety and crowd management.
